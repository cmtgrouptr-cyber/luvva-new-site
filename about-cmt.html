ARCHIVE 001

1992

The First Spark

The earliest public record of an inventive journey.

Before there was a company, there was a person who believed that a real problem deserved a real solution.

In 1992, engineer Mishrif Abu Mishrif’s first documented invention appeared in the local press. It marked the beginning of a professional path shaped by observation, practical problem-solving and the determination to turn ideas into useful solutions.

Every meaningful journey begins when someone chooses to believe that an idea can become real.

ARCHIVE 002

1993

Building the Foundation

From an idea to CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY.

In 1993, CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY began from a small electronics and electrical repair workshop.

The beginning was modest, but the approach was clear: listen carefully, understand the actual problem and build a solution that works in everyday life. Repair work became a direct study of how technology fails, how people use it and how it can be improved.

These early years formed the values that remained at the heart of the company: practical engineering, responsibility toward the user and respect for every useful idea.
