1992

Chapter One

Where the First Spark Appeared

The story did not begin with a large office, a major investment or a ready-made company. It began with an idea.

In 1992, the first officially documented invention by engineer Mishrif Abu Mishrif was reported in the local press. That moment became the earliest public record of a journey shaped by curiosity, practical problem-solving and the determination to turn ideas into useful solutions.

Every meaningful journey begins when someone chooses to believe that an idea can become real.

1993

Chapter Two

A Name, a Workshop, and a Beginning

In 1993, CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY began from a small electronics and electrical repair workshop.

It was a modest beginning, but it carried a clear way of thinking: listen carefully, understand the real problem, and build a solution that works in everyday life. Repair work became a close study of how technology fails, how people use it, and how it can be improved.

Those early years formed the character that would remain at the heart of the company: practical engineering, responsibility toward the user, and respect for the value of every idea.

The Journey Continues

Technology changed.
Markets changed.
The human purpose remained.

After more than three decades of work, learning and invention, the journey arrived at a new question:

What if technology could become more present, more caring and more human?

LUVVA is the next chapter of that journey — not a departure from the past, but a continuation of the belief that has guided CMT from the beginning: innovation matters most when it brings real value to human life.

The next chapter has not yet been written.
