ARCHIVE 001 · OFFICIAL PRESS RECORD

1992

The First Official Record

The earliest documented public recognition of an inventive journey.

The report also recorded the name behind the invention: Mishrif Abu Mishrif. It was the first official press recognition of a practical engineering journey that had already begun before the article appeared.

A protective electrical outlet designed around human safety

The 1992 article documented an electrical outlet designed to help protect children and others from electric shock if the outlet was damaged or if a metal object was inserted into one of its openings.

This was not the first invention in the journey, but it was the first to receive official press coverage and public documentation.

The newspaper did more than preserve an early invention. It introduced the public to the engineer whose work, values and persistence would later shape CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY, CMT GROUP and, decades afterward, the vision of LUVVA.

From the beginning, the purpose was clear:
technology should protect people and serve human life.

Innovation matters most when it responds to a real human need.

ARCHIVE 002

1993

Building the Foundation

From practical work to CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY.

In 1993, Mishrif Abu Mishrif transformed years of practical engineering experience into CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY, beginning from a small electronics and electrical repair workshop.

The beginning was modest, but the method was already clear: listen carefully, understand the actual problem and build a solution that works in everyday life. Repair work became a direct study of how technology fails, how people use it and how it can be improved.

These early years formed values that remained central throughout every later stage: practical engineering, responsibility toward the user and respect for every useful idea. The company would evolve, but the person carrying its original purpose forward remained the same.

Across changing countries, markets and company structures, the founder’s principles did not change. Each stage added knowledge, widened responsibility and strengthened the belief that the next chapter could serve humanity more deeply.

The journey did not end when the company name changed, when markets expanded or when its operations moved across borders.

From History to Humanity

Behind every major chapter stood the same founder — Mishrif Abu Mishrif — whose engineering experience, inventions and long-term human-centered vision continued to mature without losing their original purpose.

LUVVA did not appear overnight. It is the culmination of decades of practical work, continuous learning and one evolving vision. Mishrif Abu Mishrif is its original creator and the true driving force behind the project — the person investors will meet, question and negotiate with.

This is not a claim built on a title. It is a conclusion supported by the documented journey: the newspaper record, the inventions, CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY, CMT GROUP and the path that now reaches LUVVA.

The company evolved. The place changed. Experience deepened. The conviction only grew stronger: the most meaningful chapter is still ahead — and it should be better for humanity.
