ARCHIVE 001 · OFFICIAL PRESS RECORD

1992

The First Official Record

The earliest documented public recognition of an inventive journey.

DOCUMENTED INVENTION

A protective electrical outlet designed around human safety

The 1992 article documented an electrical outlet designed to help protect children and others from electric shock if the outlet was damaged or if a metal object was inserted into one of its openings.

This was not the first invention in the journey, but it was the first to receive official press coverage and public documentation. The article also introduced the public to the young engineer behind it: Mishrif Abu Mishrif.

The record matters not only because it preserved an invention, but because it revealed the beginning of a consistent way of thinking — identify a real human need, understand it closely and build a practical answer.

From the beginning, the purpose was clear:
technology should protect people and serve human life.

Innovation matters most when it responds to a real human need.

ARCHIVE 002

1993

Building the Foundation

From practical work to CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY.

In 1993, Mishrif Abu Mishrif transformed years of practical work and inventive thinking into CALL MIND TECHNOLOGY, beginning from a small electronics and electrical repair workshop.

The beginning was modest, but the approach was clear: listen carefully, understand the actual problem and build a solution that works in everyday life. Repair work became a direct study of how technology fails, how people use it and how it can be improved.

These early years formed values that remained central to both the founder and the company: practical engineering, responsibility toward the user and respect for every useful idea.
